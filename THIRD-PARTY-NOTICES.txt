lineage-bridge builds on two MIT-licensed projects:

  dbt-colibri — https://github.com/b-ned/dbt-colibri (Copyright (c) 2024 Canva OpenSource)
  pbip-documenter — https://github.com/JonathanJihwanKim/pbip-documenter (Copyright (c) 2026 Jihwan Kim)

dbt-colibri is used unmodified, as an external dependency.

pbip-documenter is different: its four parsers (tmdl-parser, m-parser,
visual-parser, lineage-engine) were adopted into this project as source, under
the terms of its MIT licence, and are maintained here. They carry fixes and
additions of ours and should not be read as representing upstream. Each file
names its origin and its copyright holder in a header comment.

Their full licence texts follow.
